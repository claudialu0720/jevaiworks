常见问题 / Frequently asked questions

如何开始专注？How do I start a focus session?

在湖畔主页选择时长，再轻点 Start focus。可用时长与功能以你安装的版本和权益为准。

Choose a duration on the lakeside home screen, then tap Start focus. Available durations and features depend on your installed version and access.

如何恢复 Plus 购买？How do I restore Plus purchases?

在「设置 → 会员与订阅」打开 Plus 页面，轻点底部「恢复购买」，并使用购买时的 Apple 账户。如果已经扣款但仍未解锁，请不要重复购买，欢迎邮件联系我们。恢复购买与恢复专注记录或收藏是不同操作。

Open the Plus page from Settings → Membership & subscription and tap Restore purchases at the bottom, using the Apple account used for purchase. If charged but access is missing, do not buy again; email us. Restoring purchases is separate from restoring focus history or collections.

如何管理或取消订阅？How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

月度与年度 Plus 是自动续期订阅，可在 Apple 订阅管理页面查看、管理或取消。终身方案为一次性非消耗型购买，不按月或按年续费。删除 App 或本机数据不会取消订阅；取消订阅也不会删除 App 数据。

Monthly and annual Plus plans are auto-renewable subscriptions. Review, manage or cancel them in Apple subscription management. Lifetime is a one-time, non-consumable purchase without monthly or annual renewal. Deleting the app or local data does not cancel a subscription, and cancellation does not delete app data.

本机数据如何备份或删除？How do I back up or delete local data?

在「设置 → 数据与隐私」中可导出、导入备份或选择「删除我的数据」。重置前请先保存需要的副本。删除本机数据不会删除已导出的文件，也不会自动删除已发送的第三方诊断；如需咨询已分享数据，请先复制该页显示的统计 ID 并邮件联系我们。

Settings → Data & privacy offers backup export/import and Delete my data. Save any needed copy before resetting. Local deletion does not remove exported files or automatically erase third-party diagnostics already sent. Copy the analytics ID displayed on that page before emailing us about shared data.

如何申请 Apple 购买退款？How do I request an Apple purchase refund?

可通过 Apple 问题报告页面提出退款申请。是否符合退款条件由 Apple 和适用规则决定。

Request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem page. Eligibility is determined by Apple and applicable rules.
