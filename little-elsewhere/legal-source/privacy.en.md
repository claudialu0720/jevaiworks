## 1. About this Policy

Welcome to Little Elsewhere (the “App”). Little Elsewhere is a focus timer that turns time spent concentrating into a quiet fishing experience. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process when you use the App, why we process it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

We aim to keep the App simple and privacy-conscious. **You can use its core features without creating an account. Your personal notes and detailed focus history are primarily stored on your device.** We do not sell your personal information or use your private notes for targeted advertising. You do not have to agree to non-essential analytics to use the core focus timer.

This Policy covers the App. Our website may be subject to a separate website privacy notice.

## 2. Information We Process

### 2.1 Focus activity, collections, and preferences

To make the App work, your device may create and store information such as focus-session start and end times, duration, session totals, fish caught, collected objects, achievements, map unlocks, and settings for scenes, sounds, and appearance. This information allows you to review your progress and continue your experience.

In the current version, these detailed records are stored primarily **on your device**. Starting or completing a focus session does not, by itself, mean that we upload your full focus history or collection to our own servers. Limited events relating to feature use or session outcomes may be processed separately by analytics services as explained below.

### 2.2 Inspiration notes and to-do items

Notes, to-do text, and completion status that you enter are primarily stored locally. We do not publish this content or use it for targeted advertising. Your notes are not intended to be included in analytics events or crash reports.

### 2.3 Profile and avatar

The App uses **Dulu**, our pufferfish character, as the default avatar and displays the user title **“Angler”** (with a Plus badge where applicable). The current version does not offer personal-photo avatar uploads, so we do not request access to your photo library or camera for that purpose.

### 2.4 Purchases and subscription status

When you purchase, renew, or restore a Plus subscription, the relevant app store or payment provider handles payment processing. To recognize your entitlement, the App may process necessary purchase-related details, such as a product identifier, transaction identifier, purchase verification information, subscription status, and expiry date. We do not ask you to provide your full payment-card number or payment password to us.

### 2.5 Analytics and error diagnostics

We use region-specific tools to understand basic feature usage, improve reliability, and investigate technical problems:

- **Mainland China:** Umeng+ mobile analytics.
- **Outside mainland China:** PostHog product analytics and Sentry error monitoring.

Depending on the tools and settings actually enabled, processed information may include app version, device model, operating system, network or approximate-region information, app-instance or device identifiers, feature-use events, and technical crash or diagnostic information. Network services may also receive an IP address as part of a connection. **We do not request precise GPS location for analytics**, and the language selected in the App does not, by itself, determine which regional analytics service applies.

Where consent is required for non-essential analytics, we will request it before enabling that processing. Declining or withdrawing consent does not prevent you from using the basic focus timer. Technical data strictly necessary to deliver, secure, or troubleshoot the service may be handled separately where permitted by law.

### 2.6 Communications with us

If you email us for support or to exercise a privacy right, we process the contact details and information you provide in order to respond and maintain the correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary.

## 3. Why We Use Information

We process information only for purposes relevant to operating Little Elsewhere, including:

- Providing focus timers, collections, app settings, and other requested features;
- Confirming purchases and restoring eligible subscription access;
- Understanding feature performance and fixing crashes or bugs;
- Maintaining security, preventing misuse, and complying with legal obligations;
- Responding to questions, support requests, and privacy inquiries.

Where laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, the legal basis depends on the activity: performing our contract with you (core functionality and subscription access), your consent (where required for optional analytics), our legitimate interests (limited service security and troubleshooting where lawful), or compliance with a legal obligation. We will not rely on consent where another basis is required by applicable law, or treat acceptance of this Policy as blanket consent to optional processing.

## 5. Device Permissions

The App’s core focus and fishing features do not require access to your camera, photo library, contacts, SMS messages, or precise location. If you choose to receive focus-completion reminders, we may request notification permission. You can deny or change that permission in your device settings without losing access to the timer.

An internet connection may be needed for purchases, purchase restoration, or any online features that are available. We will explain and request any additional permission before using it if future features require access to other device functions.

## 6. Storage, Retention, and Security

**Local data.** Focus records, collections, preferences, and private notes are primarily retained on your device until you delete the information, use the App’s **Clear Data** function, or the device or operating system removes the relevant storage. Clearing data may be irreversible. Uninstalling the App does not necessarily delete copies included in your device’s operating-system backups.

**Analytics and diagnostics.** Where enabled, analytics and error data are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to service configurations and applicable legal requirements. We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed, unless law requires longer retention. If you would like more detail about the retention periods applicable to information we handle, you may contact us using the details below.

**Purchase records.** Necessary transaction or entitlement information may be kept for purchase verification, the period during which benefits must be provided, dispute resolution, and applicable accounting or legal obligations. Keeping a valid purchase entitlement does not require us to keep your complete personal focus history indefinitely.

We use safeguards appropriate to the scale and risks of the service, including limiting collection and access and protecting data in transit where appropriate. No digital service can guarantee absolute security. We will respond to incidents and provide legally required notifications when necessary.

## 8. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can review or edit information available in the App and use **Settings → Clear Data** to remove the local information covered by that feature. The precise controls available depend on your App version.

Where optional analytics rely on your consent, you may withdraw that consent using the privacy controls made available in the App or by contacting us at **hello@jevaiworks.com**. Withdrawal affects future processing based on that consent; it does not necessarily undo processing already performed lawfully. Deleting local information does not automatically delete analytics or diagnostic information previously transmitted to a service provider.

Depending on the law where you live, you may have rights to access, obtain a copy of, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and in certain circumstances to request data portability. You may also have the right to complain to an appropriate data-protection authority. Contact **hello@jevaiworks.com** to exercise your rights. We may ask for reasonably necessary information to verify a request and will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

The current version does not require an account, so there is no Little Elsewhere account to delete. If account registration or cloud synchronization is introduced, we will update this Policy and explain the relevant account and deletion controls before launching those features.

## 9. Children and Minors

Little Elsewhere is not specifically directed to children under 14. Users under 18 should review this Policy with a parent or legal guardian where appropriate, and obtain guardian permission when required by law.

Under applicable Chinese law, processing the personal information of children under 14 may require parental or guardian consent and special protection measures. We will not knowingly enable non-essential personal-information analytics for such children without satisfying applicable requirements. If a parent or guardian believes a child’s information has been processed without the required consent, please contact **hello@jevaiworks.com** so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

An app-store age rating is a content classification; it does not replace applicable parental-consent or children’s privacy requirements.
