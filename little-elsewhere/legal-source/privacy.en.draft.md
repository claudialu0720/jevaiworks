Welcome to Little Elsewhere (别境, “Little Elsewhere” or “the App”). The App is developed and operated by 界外造物创意科技（青岛）有限公司 (“we”, “us”).

We value your personal information and privacy. This Policy explains how we process information when providing focus timing, fishing collections, inspiration notes, and membership services, and how you can manage that information.

**Please pay particular attention to the provisions on third-party analytics, data retention, your rights, and protection of minors.** Consent to non-essential analytics is not a condition of using basic focus features.

## I. Information we process

### 1. Focus, fishing collections, and settings

Using the App may generate and save focus start and end times, focus duration, cumulative session counts, fish and other virtual items obtained, medals, map unlock records, music and scene preferences, and similar information, so that you can view progress and continue using these features.

**In the current version, these core records are primarily stored locally on your device.** Basic features require no account registration. Starting or completing a focus session alone does not cause us to upload your complete focus records and collection contents to our own servers.

### 2. Inspiration notes and to-dos

Inspiration notes, to-do text, and completion status you enter are primarily stored locally for you to view, edit, and delete. We do not use private notes for targeted advertising or make them public without an action you initiate.

### 3. Default avatar and profile

The App uses the pufferfish character Dulu as the default avatar. Users are uniformly identified as “Angler”; eligible users may display a Plus badge. **The current version does not allow personal photo uploads as avatars and does not request photo library or camera permission for that purpose.**

### 4. Membership purchases and restoration

If you purchase or restore Plus membership, we confirm membership status through the relevant app store’s purchase interface. Necessary information, including product identifiers, purchase receipts, transaction identifiers, subscription status, and validity periods, may be processed on your device. Payment information is processed by the app store or payment provider under its rules. We do not directly request full bank card numbers or payment passwords.

### 5. Usage analytics and diagnostics

To understand basic usage, improve features, and investigate issues, we plan to use different tools by service region:

- **Mainland China:** Umeng+ mobile analytics;
- **Outside mainland China:** PostHog product analytics and Sentry error monitoring.

Depending on the actual integration configuration, these tools may process device and operating system information, network information, usage events, device or app instance identifiers, and necessary crash diagnostic data. Regional arrangements are not determined by whether you select the Chinese or English interface. We do not require precise location permission for this purpose.

**For non-essential analytics based on consent, collection will begin only after we obtain your explicit consent. You may refuse or withdraw consent and continue using basic focus features.**

## II. Device permissions

Basic focus, fishing, and record features currently do not require photo library, camera, contacts, SMS, or precise location permission.

If you choose focus completion reminders, the App may request **notification permission**. Refusing it prevents the corresponding system notifications but does not affect basic timing. You can change permissions at any time in device system settings.

Online purchases, membership restoration, and some online interactions require an internet connection. A network connection does not itself mean that we read unrelated device contents.

If future features require additional permissions, we will explain their purpose before use and obtain necessary authorization as required by law.

## IV. Storage and protection

### 1. Local data

Focus records, virtual collections, preferences, inspiration notes, and similar data are primarily stored locally, normally until you delete them, uninstalling removes local data, or the operating system clears them. Backups, restoration, and retention after uninstalling may be affected by operating system mechanisms.

You may use “Clear data” in the App to delete the local records covered by that feature. Deleted data may be unrecoverable.

### 2. Analytics and diagnostic data

We retain relevant personal information only as long as necessary for analytics or troubleshooting. Specific retention periods for third-party analytics and diagnostics depend on enabled services, dashboard configuration, and applicable law. At expiration, data will be deleted or anonymized as required by law, unless otherwise legally required.

## VI. Exercising your rights

You may view, edit, or delete local focus records, notes, and settings on the corresponding App pages, or delete covered local information through “Settings → Clear data”.

For non-essential usage analytics, manage consent under **“Settings → Privacy → Usage data analytics”**. Turning it off stops subsequent non-essential processing based on that consent; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

You may also email **hello@jevaiworks.com** to request access, copies, correction, supplementation, deletion, or restriction of processing of personal information under applicable law, or ask about applicable data portability rights. We will verify requests as necessary and handle them within statutory time limits.

**Clearing local device data does not automatically delete information previously lawfully transmitted to third parties.** Contact us at the email above for assistance with deletion or other requests concerning third-party information.

The current version requires no registration and therefore has no App account to close. If registration, cloud backup, or cross-device account services are introduced, we will update account management and closure methods accordingly.

## VII. Protection of minors

We value minors’ information security. The App is not specifically designed for children under 14. Users under 18 should read this Policy and use the services under guardian guidance.

Under Chinese law, processing personal information of children under 14 requires parental or other guardian consent and dedicated processing rules. Until required guardian consent and safeguards are in place, non-essential analytics involving such children’s information should not be enabled.

Guardians who discover that a child provided information without necessary consent may contact **hello@jevaiworks.com**. We will verify and take measures such as stopping processing and deletion as required by law.
