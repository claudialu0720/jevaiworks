帮助与支持Help & Support

需要一点帮助？

专注计时、声音或垂钓发现遇到了问题？我们愿意帮你找到答案。

Need a little help? We’re here for questions about focus sessions, sounds and fishing discoveries.

开始使用 / Getting started

如何开始一次专注？How do I start a focus session?

在湖畔主页选择专注时长，再轻点 Start focus，开始湖上时光。

Choose a duration on the lakeside home screen, then tap Start focus.

可以调整专注时长吗？Can I change the focus duration?

湖畔主页提供时长选择。开始前选择适合自己的时长；可选范围以你安装的版本为准。

Use the duration selector on the lakeside home screen before starting. Available durations depend on your installed version.

垂钓与发现 / Fishing & discoveries

如何获得鱼类和其他物品？How do I discover fish and items?

完成专注后，跟随收获画面的提示查看这次发现。不同版本的收获内容可能调整。

After a focus session, follow the discovery screen to see your find. Discoveries may vary between versions.

声音 / Sounds

如何调整音乐和环境音？How do I adjust music and ambience?

在专注界面的声音设置中，使用背景音乐、环境音开关和各声音的音量滑杆调配声景。

Open sound settings during a session. Use the music and ambience switches and individual volume sliders to adjust your soundscape.

Little Elsewhere Plus 已完成开发，团队正在完善付费界面，计划随 1.0 提供。以下为 1.0 产品方案，具体功能以正式版本为准。

Little Elsewhere Plus has been developed and its paywall is being finalized for 1.0. The following describes the 1.0 product plan; availability is subject to the release version.

如何管理或取消 Apple 订阅？How do I manage or cancel an Apple subscription?

如果你有通过 Apple 购买的订阅，可前往 Apple 订阅管理页面查看或取消。1.0 计划提供月度及年度订阅，内测可用性以安装的版本为准。删除 App 或删除 App 账户不会自动取消 Apple 订阅；取消订阅也不会删除 App 账户或数据。

If you have a subscription purchased through Apple, use Apple’s subscription management page to review or cancel it. Monthly and annual subscriptions are planned for 1.0; beta availability depends on your installed build. Deleting the app or an app account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription; canceling a subscription does not delete an app account or its data.

数据与隐私 / Data & privacy

如何备份或删除数据？How do I back up or delete data?

此前核验的内测版在「设置 → 数据与隐私」提供导出备份、导入备份和「删除我的数据」。重置前可先导出备份。重置本机数据不会删除你保存到其他位置的备份，也不会自动删除已分享的诊断信息；如需咨询请先复制该页显示的统计 ID 并邮件联系我们。此前核验的内测版无需注册账户；1.0 云端功能的账户与删除流程仍待核验。

The previously reviewed beta provides Export backup, Import backup and Delete my data in Settings → Data & privacy. Export first if you want a copy. Local reset does not delete backups saved elsewhere or automatically erase shared diagnostic data. Copy the analytics ID on that page before contacting us about it. The previously reviewed beta requires no account; account and deletion flows for 1.0 cloud features remain under verification.

如何恢复 Plus？How do I restore Plus?

使用购买时的 Apple 账户。最新开发版的入口为「设置 → 会员与订阅 → 恢复购买」（Plus 页面底部）。该流程仍需在发布候选版与实际商店环境中测试。如已扣款但未解锁，请勿重复购买，可邮件联系支持；不要发送 Apple 密码或完整付款信息。

Use the Apple account used for the purchase. In the latest development source, open Settings → Membership & subscription → Restore purchases at the bottom of the Plus page. Release-candidate and live-store testing is still required. If charged but still locked, do not buy again; contact support without sending Apple passwords or full payment details.

订阅与终身购买有什么区别？How do subscriptions and lifetime access differ?

月度、年度方案为自动续期订阅；终身方案为非消耗型 App 内购买。价格、货币、可用优惠及优惠期限以你的 App Store 商店和购买确认页面为准。介绍优惠仅适用于符合 Apple 资格要求的用户。终身方案不按月或按年续费；最终权益和恢复行为仍需发布版本验证。

Monthly and annual plans are auto-renewable subscriptions; lifetime is a non-consumable in-app purchase. Price, currency, available offers and offer duration depend on your App Store storefront and purchase confirmation. Introductory offers apply only to eligible users. Lifetime has no monthly or annual renewal; final entitlement and restoration behavior still require release-build verification.

Plus 没有解锁怎么办？What if Plus access is missing?

请核对购买使用的 Apple 账户，并在 Apple 订阅管理页面查看月度或年度订阅状态（终身购买不列为订阅）。如仍有问题，请提供 App 版本和问题说明。退款、到期及权益刷新行为正在发布版本中核验，我们不会让你为恢复同一购买而再次付费。

Check the Apple account used to purchase and review monthly/annual status in Apple subscription management (lifetime is not a subscription). If access is still missing, send support your app version and a description. Refund, expiry and entitlement refresh behavior is undergoing release-build verification; restoration should not require buying the same purchase again.
