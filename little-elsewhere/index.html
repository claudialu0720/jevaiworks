如何反馈？ How can I share feedback?

打开 TestFlight → Little Elsewhere →「发送 Beta 版反馈」。欢迎附上截图，告诉我们你的发现。

In TestFlight, select Little Elsewhere, then Send Beta Feedback. Screenshots and a few words about your experience are always welcome.

无法加入内测？ Can’t join the beta?

请用 iPhone 打开邀请，并检查 TestFlight 中显示的设备与系统要求。如果提示名额已满或暂不接受新测试员，请稍后再试。如需帮助，请联系 hello@jevaiworks.com。

Open the invitation on your iPhone and check the device and system requirements shown in TestFlight. If the beta is full or isn’t accepting testers, try again later. Need a hand? Email hello@jevaiworks.com.

目前仅限 iOS，Android 暂未开放。内测版本仍在完善，功能与画面可能调整。Currently iOS only; Android testing is not available. This is a beta, and features and visuals may change.
