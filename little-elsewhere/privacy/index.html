引言 / Introduction

欢迎来到 Little Elsewhere。我们重视你的隐私，也希望尽可能简单、透明地说明我们如何处理你的信息。本应用由界外造物创意科技（青岛）有限公司（Jevai Works，以下称“我们”）运营。公司网站的隐私政策与本 App 政策分开。

Welcome to Little Elsewhere. We care about your privacy and want to explain how your information is handled as simply and clearly as we can. The app is operated by 界外造物创意科技（青岛）有限公司 (Jevai Works, “we”). Our company website has a separate privacy policy.

审核说明：本稿依据最新 main 7137ae8 源码及负责人确认的 1.0 方案编写，不代表发布候选版已通过验证。文中“待确认”事项必须在最终发布前解决；本稿不宣称尚未核验的云同步、账户或分享功能已经可用。

Review note: this draft reflects main 7137ae8 and the owner's 1.0 decisions, not a verified release candidate. Items marked pending must be resolved before publication. Unverified cloud, account or sharing functionality is not presented as available.

一. 我们处理的信息 / 1. Information we handle

专注与个人进度：所审实现将专注时长、完成情况、目标、历史、鱼类与物品、池塘、收藏、名字和偏好保存在本机。小纸条、待办和漂流瓶相关内容也属于个人数据的一部分。Free 方案采用本机存储；Plus 的 1.0 产品方案包含云备份和跨设备同步，但最新源码文档仍将云端部分列为后续工作。待确认最终上传的字段、服务商、认证与账户信息、同步方式和删除范围，不作“全部数据永不离开设备”的承诺。

Focus and progress: the reviewed implementation keeps focus duration, completion, goals, history, fish/items, Pond, collections, display name and preferences locally. Notes, to-dos and bottle-related content form part of your personal data. Free uses local storage. The Plus 1.0 plan includes cloud backup and cross-device sync, while current source documentation still places cloud work in a later stage. Final uploaded fields, providers, authentication/accounts, sync and deletion scope remain pending. We do not promise that all data always stays on-device.

账户：所审源码没有客户注册或登录系统；本机名字不是在线账户，友盟的统计 ID 也不是客户登录。若 1.0 云端功能引入账户，最终政策须补充实际标识、登录服务和账户删除方式。

Accounts: no customer-registration or login system was found in the reviewed source. A local display name and Umeng analytics ID are not customer accounts. If 1.0 cloud features introduce accounts, the final policy must identify their actual identifiers, login providers and deletion flow.

可选统计与诊断：开启“帮助改进别境”后，友盟+ 会处理设备与网络信息、设备型号、系统/应用版本、网络类型、IP 地址、IDFV、UMID，以及应用生成的随机统计 ID。项目集成记录确认诊断报告含 IP 推断的城市级位置。这些标识用于归集报告，属于假名化标识，不能称为完全匿名。

Optional analytics and diagnostics: with Help improve Little Elsewhere enabled, Umeng+ processes device/network information, model, OS/app version, network type, IP address, IDFV, UMID and an app-generated random analytics ID. Integration records confirm an IP-derived city in diagnostic reports. These identifiers group reports and are pseudonymous, not fully anonymous.

使用事件包括介绍、页面停留、专注开始/完成/提前结束、保留收获、池塘和图鉴、分享与反馈入口、恢复专注和保存失败。参数包括场景、语言、版本、计划/实际时长、是否填写目标、提醒状态、离开 App 的次数、收获种类及稀有度；不将目标或笔记文字作为这些事件的参数。错误报告含程序堆栈、错误类型、经过清理的错误描述和场景上下文。最终二进制、SDK 自动收集字段和新增购买/分享事件仍须核验。

Usage events cover onboarding, page duration, focus start/completion/early exit, kept catches, Pond/Collection, share/feedback entry, recovery and save failures. Attributes include scene, language, version, planned/actual duration, whether a goal is present, reminder state, app departures and catch kind/rarity—not goal or note text. Errors include stacks, type, sanitized descriptions and scene context. The final binary, automatic SDK fields and any new purchase/sharing events still require verification.

二. 我们如何使用信息及你的选择 / 2. Uses and choices

本机信息用于保存进度、展示记录和提供专注与收集体验；购买权益用于识别 Plus；统计和诊断用于了解功能使用、排查错误和改善稳定性。你主动发送的反馈用于回复和处理问题。云同步及公开分享的具体用途和处理依据须随最终实现补全，不以笼统“可能收集”代替说明。

Local information saves progress and supports focus, collection and history. Purchase entitlements identify Plus access. Analytics and diagnostics help understand features and fix stability problems. Feedback you send is used to respond and investigate. The purposes and processing basis for cloud sync and public sharing must be completed against their final implementation rather than covered by vague possible collection.

隐私提示中的“同意并继续”会开启“帮助改进别境”；“仅使用基本功能”保持关闭。你可在“设置 → 数据与隐私”调整该开关。所审代码在同意前不初始化友盟；关闭后停止 App 主动提交的事件和错误报告。

Agree & Continue enables Help improve Little Elsewhere; Basic features only leaves it off. You can change it in Settings → Data & privacy. Reviewed code does not initialize Umeng before consent and stops app-submitted events and errors when disabled.

发布前待解决：当前 U-APM 无运行中关闭原生崩溃捕获的接口，关闭开关后本次进程仍可能记录原生崩溃；关闭分享的下一次启动不会初始化 SDK，缓存报告可能在将来重新开启时上传。因此不能承诺“关闭后立即停止全部收集和传送”。开发者须解决或完成合规评估，并核验实际设备行为、同意界面和最终政策一致。

Release issue: current U-APM cannot disable native crash capture mid-process. Capture may persist until the process closes; a later launch with sharing off does not initialize it, while cached reports may upload if sharing is later enabled. We cannot promise that disabling immediately stops all collection/transmission. The developer must resolve or assess this issue and align device behavior, consent UI and final policy.

三. 小纸条与漂流瓶 / 3. Notes and drift bottles

你写下的小纸条属于个人内容。所审本机实现不因你写下文字就把它发布给其他用户。1.0 方案中的扩展漂流瓶分享和自定义仍需核验接收者、展示身份、上传内容、保存期限和审核流程，不能把接收预置瓶中文字或系统分享图片视为已实现用户间公开交流。

Your notes are personal content. In the reviewed local implementation, writing text does not itself publish it to other users. The 1.0 plan's extended bottle sharing/customization still needs verification of recipients, displayed identity, uploaded content, retention and moderation. Receiving bundled bottle text or sharing an image through the OS is not proof of an implemented public user-to-user feature.

若最终提供公开分享，只应在你主动选择后展示相应内容，并在使用前清楚说明可见范围。请不要在公开内容中填写身份证号、电话、住址、密码或他人的隐私信息。相关举报、屏蔽、审核、撤回和删除能力须在上线前核实，不能仅凭本条文字认为已具备这些功能。

Any final public-sharing feature should display content only after your deliberate choice and explain its audience first. Please keep ID numbers, phone numbers, addresses, passwords and other people's private information out of public content. Reporting, blocking, moderation, withdrawal and deletion must be verified before launch; this text does not establish that those tools exist.

四. 数据保存、删除与其他权利 / 4. Storage, deletion and rights

本机记录保留供你使用。“设置 → 数据与隐私”提供导出、导入和“删除我的数据”。导出使用本地文件与 iOS 系统分享面板；副本可能被保存到你选择的文件或云服务，请自行妥善保管。重置本机数据不会删除这些外部副本，也不等同于账户注销、云端删除或取消订阅。

Local records remain available for your use. Settings → Data & privacy provides export, import and Delete my data. Export uses a local file and the iOS share sheet; copies may go to your chosen file/cloud service, so protect them. Local reset does not remove external copies, close an account, delete cloud records or cancel a subscription.

删除本机数据会重置玩法记录和统计 ID，并保留隐私选择。它不会自动删除友盟已收到的诊断。若需要查询或请求删除已分享的数据，请先复制页面上的统计 ID 再联系我们。待确认服务商按 ID 查询/删除的能力、历史 ID 匹配、可删除范围与处理时限；不承诺立即删除。对于法定保留或技术上不能立即删除的情况，应依法说明范围与原因并限制后续处理，不能作为无限保留的理由。

Local deletion resets experience data and the analytics ID while retaining the privacy choice. It does not automatically erase diagnostics already received by Umeng. Copy the displayed analytics ID before contacting us about shared data. Provider access/deletion by ID, historical-ID matching, scope and response times remain pending; immediate deletion is not promised. Any legal retention or technical deletion limit must be explained and further processing limited as required, rather than used to justify indefinite retention.

你可通过 App 查看和修改本机名字、笔记及设置，并导出玩法副本；查询、更正、删除、撤回同意或其他适用权利也可邮件联系。待批准：各类统计、崩溃、购买缓存、云端记录、支持邮件和备份的具体保留规则、处理依据、处理时限以及服务商清理流程。

You can view/change local names, notes and settings and export experience data. Email us about access, correction, deletion, withdrawal or other applicable rights. Retention rules, processing basis, response times and provider cleanup for analytics, crashes, purchase caches, cloud records, support mail and backups await approval.

五. 第三方、购买与处理地点 / 5. Providers, purchases and locations

友盟+：iOS 原生集成固定 UMCommon 7.7.0、UMDevice 3.6.0、UMAPM 2.0.7，用于 U-App 统计和 U-APM 诊断。项目还列有 U-APM 内含的啄木鸟组件；其关联政策提供方为广州市动景计算机科技有限公司。友盟当前合同主体、最终包的完整组件/字段及适用供应商条款待确认。友盟政策入口；关联诊断服务政策。供应商通用政策不等于本应用的具体配置证明。

Umeng+: the iOS integration pins UMCommon 7.7.0, UMDevice 3.6.0 and UMAPM 2.0.7 for U-App analytics/U-APM diagnostics. The project also identifies a Woodpecker component in U-APM; its associated policy names Guangzhou Dongjing Computer Technology Co., Ltd. (广州市动景计算机科技有限公司). The current Umeng contracting entity, final bundled components/fields and applicable vendor terms need confirmation. See Umeng policy and associated diagnostic policy. General vendor policies do not prove this app's configuration.

Apple 购买：月度和年度 Plus 为自动续期订阅，终身 Plus 为非消耗型购买。所审实现用 expo-iap 6.3.0 调用 StoreKit 2 获取产品与权益、恢复和完成交易，购买适配器不接入 RevenueCat 或自建验证服务器。本机缓存方案、到期时间、续期状态和最近核验时间；此缓存独立于玩法备份。Apple 按其规则处理 App Store 购买、付款与退款；本购买适配器未实现读取完整银行卡信息。最终交易字段、保留和其他接收方仍需发布包核验。

Apple purchases: monthly/annual Plus are auto-renewable subscriptions; lifetime Plus is non-consumable. Reviewed code uses expo-iap 6.3.0 with StoreKit 2 to obtain products/entitlements, restore and finish transactions, without RevenueCat or a custom validation server in that adapter. Plan, expiry, renewal state and last-check time are cached separately from experience backups. Apple handles App Store billing/refunds under its rules; this adapter does not implement access to full card details. Final transaction fields, retention and any other recipients still need release-binary verification.

其他功能：Expo/React Native 提供本地音频、文件选择、分享和通知等能力。所审通知用于本地专注提醒，不能据此宣称使用远程推送服务；复制按钮写入剪贴板，不能据此宣称读取剪贴板。你主动发出的支持邮件由邮件服务处理，其服务商与保留规则待确认。

Other functions: Expo/React Native provide local audio, file picking, sharing and notifications. Reviewed reminders are local, not evidence of remote push. Copy controls write to the clipboard, not evidence of reading it. Support mail you send is processed by email services; the providers and retention rules remain pending.

处理地点与安全：本机数据与由 SDK 发往服务器的数据应区分。不得仅凭项目意向保证所有信息在中国境内或永不跨境。待确认生产服务器地区、云服务和 SDK 接收方、跨境安排、访问控制、传输/缓存保护及安全事件响应。所审代码采用同意门控、有限事件参数和错误清理；不将这些措施表述为已验证的全面加密或绝对安全保证。

Locations/security: distinguish device-local data from SDK server processing. Project intentions do not establish China-only storage or no international transfers. Production regions, cloud/SDK recipients, transfer arrangements, access control, transport/cache protections and incident response remain pending. Reviewed code gates consent, limits event fields and sanitizes errors; these measures are not a verified claim of comprehensive encryption or absolute security.

六. 儿童隐私 / 6. Children's privacy

别境不专门面向儿童，拟申请效率类别、4+ 内容评级，不选择儿童类别。内容评级不等于隐私政策最低用户年龄。中国大陆对不满十四周岁未成年人的个人信息实行敏感个人信息保护，相关处理需要监护人同意及专门规则等措施。当前未核验年龄识别或监护人同意机制，不能声称已满足这些要求；仅在文字中要求监护人同意不足以证明机制存在。

Little Elsewhere is not specifically designed for children; the intended category is Productivity with a 4+ content rating, outside Kids Category. A content rating is not a privacy minimum age. Mainland China treats under-14 personal information as sensitive and requires guardian consent and dedicated rules/protections for relevant processing. No age-assurance or guardian-consent mechanism has been verified. A sentence requesting consent does not prove such a mechanism exists.

负责人及法律审核须决定最低年龄、未成年人使用和可选遥测/云端/分享的可用范围，并完成必要产品措施。若发现未经适当授权处理儿童信息，应提供停止、联系与删除处理路径；具体流程需批准后写入最终政策，不宣称当前已经完成。

The owner/legal reviewer must decide minimum age, minor access and the scope of optional telemetry/cloud/sharing, and complete necessary product measures. Unauthorized child-data processing needs a workable stop/contact/deletion procedure. Its approved implementation must be reflected in the final policy, rather than claimed complete here.

七. 政策更新 / 7. Updates

随着别境的发展，我们可能更新本政策。重大变化将通过 App 内提示或其他适当方式说明，并在依法需要时重新取得同意。所审 App 有政策版本提示机制；最终网站、App 文本和 App Store 披露须保持一致。

As Little Elsewhere grows, we may update this policy. Material changes will be explained in-app or through another appropriate channel, with renewed consent where required. Reviewed code includes a policy-version prompt; final website/app text and App Store disclosures must agree.
