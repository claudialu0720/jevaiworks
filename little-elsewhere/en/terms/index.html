5. What happens when Plus ends

If a monthly or annual subscription ends, you can continue using the free features. Plus-only features return to their free-tier limits, but the catches and other local progress you already earned are not automatically deleted.

For example, if you have 20 fish in your Pond when Plus ends, all 20 remain. When the Pond contains 10 or more fish, you cannot increase its total count. However, a newly caught fish awaiting a Pond decision can still replace an existing fish one-for-one through “Edit Pond”. When the count falls below the free-tier limit of 10, you may add fish again until it reaches 10. Removing one fish from 20 leaves 19 and therefore does not allow direct additions from your Collection, although a one-for-one replacement of a pending catch remains available.

Maps previously unlocked with BUBS remain unlocked while the corresponding local app data is retained, even if Plus ends. Data loss and restoration limitations are described in Section 3.
