Little Elsewhere Privacy Policy

Effective date　November 9, 2026

This policy applies to Little Elsewhere for iOS. You can use this focus app without a Little Elsewhere account. Your detailed focus history and game progress are stored primarily on your device. Plus members can back up and restore their data through their own iCloud account. Optional usage analytics and diagnostics start only after you opt in. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.

2. Local data, export and iCloud backup

Little Elsewhere stores your focus history, durations and goals, inspiration notes, to-dos and message bottle contents, catches, Collection, Pond and badges, BUBS balance and unlocked maps, and your chosen name and preferences on your device. These detailed records support the app’s features. We have no servers of our own that receive these complete records. Section 4 describes the limited usage events that may be sent if you opt in.

You can use Export a backup in Settings › Data & privacy to save a readable copy wherever you choose or send it to a recipient through your device’s share menu. The public App Store version supports export. Restoring from an exported file is currently unavailable in that version.

On iOS, the app’s iCloud backup is on by default for Plus members. When iCloud is available, the app stores the latest snapshot of your progress as a signed backup file in the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. The record also contains the backup time, app version, device model and file size. Our systems cannot read your private backup. You can turn off automatic backup in Settings › Data & privacy, or select Back up now to create a backup manually. After your Plus membership ends, you can still restore an existing backup from the same iCloud account. Restoring replaces the progress on that device.

This feature restores progress from a snapshot and is managed separately from your operating system’s backups. It does not keep progress synchronized across devices. In mainland China, iCloud is operated by Guizhou-Cloud Big Data (GCBD). Apple or GCBD handles iCloud data under its respective policy. Apple Privacy Policy · GCBD iCloud Privacy Policy

If you use an operating-system backup such as iCloud device backup or a computer backup, it may also retain a copy of your app data. Uninstalling the app or using Delete my data removes data from the app’s local storage. Exported files, the app’s iCloud backup and operating-system backups are managed separately wherever they are stored. The app’s iCloud backup setting and your choice about optional analytics and diagnostics operate independently.

3. Regional services and your consent

The app first checks the country or region of your App Store storefront. If that check fails or times out, it uses, in order, the region saved from the last successful check, your device’s region setting, and the app language resolved from your system language. The app uses at most one group of optional analytics and diagnostics services per launch. The mainland China route uses Umeng+, and the other regions route uses PostHog and Sentry.

This route follows storefront or device settings and may differ from your physical location. For example, if you use an overseas App Store storefront while in mainland China and opt in, analytics and diagnostics data may be sent to Germany. Your route does not determine which data protection laws apply.

The app enables optional analytics and diagnostics for the selected route only after you select Agree & Continue on the privacy prompt or turn on Help improve Little Elsewhere in Settings › Data & privacy. The same switch controls both uses. You can choose Basic features only and still use core features such as the focus timer. If the app switches service groups, it asks for your consent before enabling the new group.

If you opt in, we use the following information to understand how features are used, improve the app and troubleshoot problems. Fields vary by provider, event and app version.

6. Storage locations, IP addresses and retention

Umeng+ states that it stores data for its SDK services for mainland Chinese developers in mainland China. Yueying’s public policy states that it generally stores information generated or collected in mainland China there and may transfer some information abroad under applicable conditions. Our PostHog and Sentry projects are configured for the EU region in Germany. Optional data may be transferred across borders or accessed from another country depending on the route and how the provider handles it. Umeng+ Privacy Policy · Yueying SDK Privacy Policy

Analytics data processed by Umeng: Umeng retains personal information for the period necessary to provide its analytics services, maintain relevant service records, and ensure service security and quality, unless a longer retention period is required by law or otherwise agreed. After the applicable retention period, Umeng states that it deletes or anonymizes the personal information. See Umeng's Privacy Policy.

U-APM and Yueying SDK diagnostics. The public policies of Umeng+ and Yueying SDK describe retaining personal information for as long as needed for purposes such as diagnostics and service safeguards. Each policy describes when longer retention may apply and how information is deleted or anonymized at the end of the applicable period. Umeng+ Privacy Policy · Yueying SDK Privacy Policy

PostHog analytics. Our project retains analytics data for 12 months. The project is configured to discard client IP addresses, and the app disables PostHog’s location lookup from IP addresses. PostHog data storage documentation

Sentry diagnostics. Our project retains diagnostics data for 30 days. We enabled settings to prevent Sentry from storing IP addresses. The app’s JavaScript reporting path also strips user IP address, email, username and request data fields. Service providers may encounter IP addresses in the course of receiving network requests, and the SDK handles native diagnostics separately. Sentry Privacy Policy

Local data stays on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app or the operating system removes it. A new in-app iCloud backup replaces the previous snapshot. Turning off backup or deleting local data leaves an existing iCloud copy in place. Retention of exports, system backups and iCloud data depends on how you manage them and on the relevant services. We retain correspondence about feedback and privacy requests for as long as necessary to handle them and meet applicable legal obligations.

7. Withdrawing consent, deletion and other rights

You can turn off Help improve Little Elsewhere in Settings › Data & privacy. The app immediately stops submitting new usage events and error reports. PostHog’s local queue of events waiting to be sent is cleared. Sentry may send queued reports as it closes. Native crash capture that has already started, including Umeng+ U-APM’s native capture, may continue until the app fully exits. The app leaves the optional SDKs off on subsequent launches unless you opt in again. Withdrawal leaves earlier processing based on consent in place and does not automatically erase data already processed or diagnostics retained by an SDK.

You can view your progress, change your display name and preferences, or use Export a backup to get a copy of your local data. Delete my data in Settings › Data & privacy clears local data. If optional sharing is still on, the app creates a new analytics ID. If sharing is off, it clears the old ID and creates a new one only if you opt in later. Turning sharing off alone leaves your existing ID in place. Deleting local data does not automatically delete backups or records already received by service providers. There is no Little Elsewhere account to close.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, copy, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to its processing, or complain to a competent authority. Contact us at hello@jevaiworks.com. We will verify and handle requests under applicable law. If your analytics ID is shown in Data & privacy, you can copy the full ID and include it to help us locate records. You can still make a request if your ID is unavailable or has been reset. If we cannot locate records using the information available, we will explain the limitation and any available next steps.
