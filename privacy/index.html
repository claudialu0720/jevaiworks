网站隐私政策Website Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-11-03

本政策适用于界外造物创意科技（青岛）有限公司（以下简称「我们」）的公司官网 www.jevaiworks.com。

This policy applies to www.jevaiworks.com, the company website operated by 界外造物创意科技（青岛）有限公司 (Jevai Works, “we”, “us”).

网站访问 / Visiting this website

本网站用于介绍公司及产品，不提供账户注册、在线支付或信息提交表单。字体和图片随网站本身提供。以下网站访问统计说明也适用于 Little Elsewhere 中英文介绍页、帮助页及网站上的法律说明页面。

This informational website has no account registration, payments or submission forms. Fonts and images are served with the site. The website analytics disclosure below also covers the Chinese and English Little Elsewhere landing pages, the support page and legal information pages on this website.

访问统计与 Cookie / Analytics & cookies

我们使用 Google Analytics 4（GA4）了解每天的访问人数、页面浏览量、访问来源以及页面互动，以改进网站内容。Google 的统计脚本可能使用 Cookie 和浏览器标识符，处理访问页面及其网址、来源页面、浏览器和设备信息、互动事件及大致地理位置等数据。请勿通过网址参数提交姓名、邮箱或其他个人信息。

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand daily visitor numbers, page views, traffic sources and page interactions, and to improve the website. Google's analytics script may use cookies and browser identifiers and process page URLs, referrers, browser and device information, interaction events and approximate location. Please do not submit names, email addresses or other personal information through URL parameters.

官网与 Little Elsewhere 页面分别发送至对应的网站数据流，每页仅配置一个衡量 ID。代码已关闭 Google signals 和广告个性化信号。Google 按其服务配置处理统计数据，可能涉及境外处理；详情见 Google 如何使用合作伙伴网站或应用的数据及 Google 隐私政策。

Company and Little Elsewhere pages send analytics to their respective web data streams, with one measurement ID configured per page. Google signals and advertising personalization signals are disabled in the code. Google processes analytics data according to its service configuration, which may involve processing outside your country. See How Google uses information from partner sites or apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

你可以通过浏览器设置管理或清除 Cookie，也可以使用 Google 提供的 Analytics 停用浏览器插件阻止支持的浏览器向 Google Analytics 分享访问数据。仅禁用 Cookie 不一定阻止所有统计请求。

You can manage or clear cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent supported browsers from sharing visit data with Google Analytics. Blocking cookies alone may not prevent all analytics requests.

托管服务 / Hosting

本网站计划通过 GitHub Pages 提供。网站上线后，托管服务可能处理提供网页及保障服务安全所需的技术信息，例如 IP 地址和请求日志。有关 GitHub 对此类信息的处理，请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。

This website is prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. Once hosted, the provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its data practices.

主动联系我们 / Contacting us

如果你通过公布的联系方式主动联系我们，我们会使用你提供的邮箱、姓名和消息内容回复咨询、处理合作或产品问题。请勿在初次联系中发送不必要的敏感个人信息。我们仅在处理相应事宜及履行适用义务所需的时间内保留这些联系信息。

If you contact us using a published contact channel, we use the name, email address and message you provide to respond and handle your enquiry. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. We retain correspondence only for as long as needed to address the matter and meet applicable obligations.

关于 Little Elsewhere / About the app

本政策说明公司官网及产品介绍网页的处理方式，不是 Little Elsewhere（别境）App 内部数据处理的隐私政策。App 隐私说明请参阅独立的应用隐私政策页面及其当前版本状态。

This policy covers the company website and product landing pages. It does not describe data processing inside the Little Elsewhere app. For the app, see the separate app privacy policy page and its current version status.

联系与更新 / Contact & updates

如需咨询本政策，或提出有关你向我们提供的信息的访问、更正、删除请求，请使用官网联系栏目公布的渠道。网站功能或数据处理方式改变时，我们会相应更新本政策及日期。

For questions about this policy or requests to access, correct or delete information you have shared with us, use the channel published in our contact section. We will update this policy and its date when the website or its data practices change.
